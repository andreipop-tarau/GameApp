Retention System

12.1 Purpose

The Retention System is designed to encourage players to return
regularly through meaningful goals, fresh content, and satisfying
progression. It must avoid manipulative practices that create
frustration or unfairness.

12.2 Core Retention Principles

-   Instant access to gameplay
-   Short sessions
-   Continuous progression
-   Fair competition
-   Frequent cosmetic rewards
-   Fresh daily content

12.3 Habit Loop

The intended player loop:

Open App → Play Challenge → Receive Reward → View Progress → Unlock Goal
→ Play Again

The loop should remain fast and rewarding.

12.4 Variable Rewards

Rewards may include: - XP - Brain Chips - Cosmetics - Achievements -
Collection progress - Seasonal progress

The timing of cosmetic rewards should remain varied to maintain
excitement.

12.5 Near-Miss Design

Players should frequently experience: - Narrow victories - Narrow
defeats - Personal best improvements

Near misses should motivate another attempt without feeling unfair.

12.6 Daily Motivation

Players should always find: - Daily Challenge - Daily Missions - Daily
Shop - Free Lucky Spin - Login Reward

12.7 Long-Term Goals

Long-term objectives include: - Complete collections - Reach higher
levels - Finish achievements - Improve Brain Profile - Earn seasonal
rewards

12.8 Notifications

Optional notifications: - Daily Challenge available - Shop refreshed -
Event started - Friend beat your score - Season ending soon

Players may disable notifications at any time.

12.9 Ethical Design

Avoid: - Forced waiting - Energy systems - Paywalls - Artificial
difficulty spikes - Misleading rewards

Retention should come from enjoyment rather than frustration.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Daily content refreshes automatically.
-   Players always have at least one meaningful objective.
-   Notifications remain optional.
-   Progression supports long-term engagement.
-   Retention systems never create pay-to-win mechanics.

End of Chapter 12.

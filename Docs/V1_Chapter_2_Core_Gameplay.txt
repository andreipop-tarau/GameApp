Volume 1 - Chapter 2

Core Gameplay

2.1 Gameplay Loop

The gameplay loop is designed around speed and repetition.

Open App → Tap Play → Challenge Starts → Win or Lose → Results (<1
second) → Instant Restart

The complete loop should feel frictionless.

2.2 Round Structure

Each round contains: 1. Challenge initialization. 2. Active gameplay. 3.
Immediate evaluation. 4. Rewards and statistics. 5. Restart.

Rounds should last between 5 and 30 seconds.

2.3 Controls

Supported interactions: - Tap - Double Tap - Hold - Swipe - Drag -
Multi-touch (future)

Controls must be responsive with minimal latency.

2.4 Challenge Duration

The AI selects a target duration based on player skill: - Easy: 5–10
seconds - Medium: 10–20 seconds - Hard: 20–30 seconds

2.5 Win Conditions

Examples: - Survive until timer ends. - Remember the correct sequence. -
Tap the correct object. - Ignore misleading instructions. - Solve a
logic task. - Predict movement.

Every challenge clearly defines success.

2.6 Lose Conditions

Examples: - Wrong selection. - Time expires. - Touching hazards. -
Following deceptive instructions. - Missing required actions.

Failure should always feel understandable and fair.

2.7 Feedback System

Winning: - Positive sound. - Haptic feedback. - XP gained. - Brain
Profile updated. - Progress animations.

Losing: - Immediate explanation when appropriate. - Highlight mistake. -
Encourage instant replay.

2.8 Restart System

Restart must require one tap.

No loading screen.

Target restart time: Less than 500 milliseconds.

The player should always be able to start another round immediately.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Gameplay starts within 2 seconds of pressing Play.
-   Restart is nearly instant.
-   Controls remain responsive at 60 FPS.
-   Every round can be completed with one hand.
-   No unnecessary menus interrupt the gameplay loop.

End of Chapter 2.

Volume 1 - Chapter 7

Economy System

7.1 Purpose

The Economy System provides long-term motivation while remaining fair.
All economy design must support unlimited free play and avoid pay-to-win
mechanics.

7.2 Brain Chips

Brain Chips are the premium currency.

Sources: - Purchase - Events - Achievements - Limited gameplay rewards -
Referrals

7.3 Currency Packs

Founder Event: - €1 = 300 Chips - €3 = 1100 Chips - €5 = 2100 Chips -
€10 = 5000 Chips

Standard: - €1 = 150 Chips - €3 = 550 Chips - €5 = 1050 Chips - €10 =
2500 Chips

7.4 Reward Sources

Players earn free Chips through: - Daily login - Daily challenge -
Weekly challenge - Achievements - Events - Season milestones

7.5 Chip Sinks

Brain Chips may purchase: - Themes - Frames - Particles - Badges - Name
effects - Hint Tokens - Continue Tokens - Lucky Spins - Cosmetic bundles

7.6 Daily Rewards

Consecutive logins increase rewards. Missing one day resets the streak
unless protected by an event.

7.7 Referral Rewards

Inviter and invited player both receive Brain Chips after account
verification.

7.8 Economy Rules

-   Unlimited gameplay.
-   Never sell power.
-   Cosmetics remain the primary sink.
-   Rare items rotate through the shop.

7.9 Anti-Inflation

Reward values are configurable from the backend. Events should not
permanently inflate the economy.

7.10 Founder Event

Duration: 30 days. Benefits: - 100% Bonus Chips - Founder Badge -
Founder Theme - Founder Frame - Founder Particle Effect

Acceptance Criteria

-   Economy is configurable.
-   No gameplay advantages from purchases.
-   Premium and free players compete equally.
-   Chip earning remains balanced long-term.

End of Chapter 7.

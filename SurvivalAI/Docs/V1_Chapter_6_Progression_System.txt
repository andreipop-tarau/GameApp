Progression System

6.1 Purpose

The Progression System rewards continued play without affecting gameplay
balance. All progression is cosmetic, social, or completion-based.

6.2 Experience (XP)

Players earn XP by: - Completing challenges - Winning rounds - Daily
challenges - Weekly challenges - Achievements - Events

XP is never purchasable.

6.3 Player Levels

Levels represent dedication rather than skill.

Level rewards may include: - Themes - Avatar frames - Profile badges -
Titles - Brain Chips - Seasonal rewards

Higher levels never provide gameplay advantages.

6.4 Achievements

Achievement categories: - Gameplay - Challenge mastery - Daily
participation - Weekly participation - Social - Founder - Seasonal -
Hidden - Impossible

Achievements grant XP, cosmetics or Brain Chips.

6.5 Collections

Players can collect: - Themes - Frames - Particles - Badges - Name
effects - Titles - Event cosmetics

Collections encourage long-term engagement.

6.6 Missions

Daily Missions: - Play rounds - Complete challenge categories - Reach
streaks

Weekly Missions: - Higher targets - Larger rewards

Mission progress resets automatically.

6.7 Seasons

Each season introduces: - New cosmetics - New achievements - New
missions - Limited rewards - Seasonal leaderboard

Completed seasonal rewards remain permanently owned.

6.8 Reward Philosophy

Rewards should occur frequently enough to maintain motivation while
preserving the value of rare items.

Rare cosmetics should remain aspirational.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Progression never creates pay-to-win mechanics.
-   XP is earned only through gameplay.
-   Levels unlock cosmetic or social rewards only.
-   Daily and weekly missions refresh automatically.
-   Seasonal progression supports future expansion.

End of Chapter 6.

Volume 1 - Chapter 9

Monetization System

9.1 Purpose

Monetization must support long-term development while preserving player
trust. All purchases are optional and must never provide competitive
advantages.

9.2 Core Principles

-   Unlimited free gameplay
-   No Pay-to-Win
-   Cosmetics first
-   Low-friction purchases
-   High perceived value
-   Transparent pricing

9.3 Purchase Psychology

The shop should encourage many small purchases rather than a few
expensive ones.

Goals: - Affordable entry point - Frequent cosmetic releases - Valuable
bundles - Limited-time offers

9.4 Founder Event

Duration: 30 days after launch.

Benefits: - 100% Bonus Brain Chips - Founder Badge - Founder Theme -
Founder Avatar Frame - Founder Particle Effect

The event countdown should be visible throughout the application.

9.5 Starter Offers

New players receive: - Starter Bundle - Limited introductory pricing -
One-time purchase

Starter offers disappear after purchase or expiration.

9.6 Battle Pass

Cosmetics only.

Contains: - Themes - Frames - Particles - Titles - Badges

No gameplay advantages.

9.7 Limited-Time Offers

Examples: - Weekend bundles - Holiday collections - Anniversary
cosmetics - Seasonal bundles

Offers rotate automatically through LiveOps.

9.8 Revenue Metrics

Track: - Conversion rate - Average revenue per paying user - Average
revenue per active user - Bundle popularity - Purchase frequency -
Cosmetic ownership

9.9 Player Trust

Never: - Sell power - Hide prices - Manipulate difficulty - Force
purchases - Lock gameplay behind payment

Maintain a fair competitive environment.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Every purchase is optional.
-   Cosmetic purchases update instantly.
-   Founder Event is configurable remotely.
-   Battle Pass remains cosmetic-only.
-   Revenue systems do not affect gameplay balance.

End of Chapter 9.

# Product vision

MindTrap AI is a fast, funny, one-handed mobile game about learning fair tricks and surviving short interactive microgames. The primary experience is Troll Gauntlet; Calm Run and Rush Run reuse a separate one-touch runner foundation. Core play is unlimited, local-first, seeded, deterministic, and never pay-to-win.

## Product promise

1. Reach meaningful interaction within seconds and restart immediately.
2. Make every objective understandable in about one second.
3. Surprise the player with visible, learnable rules rather than technical deception.
4. Keep the application shell calm and premium; concentrate absurdity inside gameplay.
5. Reward mastery, personal-best improvement, learned troll tells, and cosmetic expression.
6. Keep ordinary play offline. Server authority is reserved for future competition, currency, inventory, and purchases.
7. Treat accessibility, touch accuracy, lifecycle safety, performance, and honest failure explanations as game rules.

"AI" means a deterministic adaptive director over shipped content and local player history. Any target success band is a tunable design hypothesis, not a scientific or medical claim. No LLM or online inference belongs in the round loop.

## Connected modes

- **Troll Gauntlet:** the primary 4-6 minute run of 5-15 second microgames, escalating modifiers, three lives, and a rare late boss.
- **Calm Run:** a 60-90 second no-pressure one-touch runner with predictable rhythm, non-terminal collisions, optional feedback, and a gradual cooldown.
- **Rush Run:** a faster one-touch runner focused on distance, combos, instant restart, personal bests, and cosmetics.

`Flow Run` is the internal shared runner architecture for Calm Run and Rush Run, not a fourth player-facing mode.

## Audience and product evidence

The primary audience is players aged 13+ who enjoy quick physical comedy, fair misdirection, short mastery loops, and one-handed mobile play. Secondary audiences are personal-best chasers and cosmetic collectors.

Measure first-run interaction time, objective comprehension, retry rate, run completion, personal-best improvement, genuine-tell recognition, D1/D7 retention, crash-free sessions, frame performance, and accessibility failures. Numeric targets remain hypotheses until playtesting supplies evidence.

## Ethical boundaries

Never add energy, forced waiting, paid lives, mandatory ads, loot boxes, hidden odds, fake scarcity, missed-streak punishment, manipulated losing streaks, paid gameplay power, or purchases shown as an emotional response to failure.

Never use hidden hitboxes, random input lag, unavoidable failure, fake operating-system permission screens, fake purchases/device warnings, or punishment for leaving the app. Discovery bosses may imitate an in-game malfunction only inside the clearly bounded game scene and may not imitate system UI.

Do not present Calm Run, Brain Profile, or any other feature as diagnosis or treatment for anxiety, stress, intelligence, or health.

## Original identity

MindTrap AI uses a restrained shell and an original family of rounded geometric creatures called **Traplings**. Their silhouettes, expressions, object transformations, short sound motifs, and consistent broken-circle troll-tell language carry the brand. The product may learn from broad qualities of premium mobile software and one-touch rhythm games, but it must not copy another product's branding, layouts, assets, levels, characters, music, or proprietary visual patterns.

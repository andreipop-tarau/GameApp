Challenge Generation

Purpose

Generate unique gameplay sessions from reusable challenge modules.

Pipeline

-   Select category
-   Choose mechanic
-   Apply difficulty
-   Randomize parameters
-   Validate playability

Rules

-   No impossible combinations.
-   High replayability.
-   Deterministic from configuration.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Challenges are valid.
-   Minimal repetition.
-   Supports future modules.

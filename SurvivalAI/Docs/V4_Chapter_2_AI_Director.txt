Volume 4 - Chapter 2

AI Director

Purpose

The AI Director personalizes gameplay by selecting challenges, adjusting
difficulty, and maintaining player engagement.

Inputs

-   Brain Profile
-   Recent performance
-   Session length
-   Streaks
-   Fatigue indicators

Responsibilities

-   Select challenge category
-   Tune difficulty
-   Avoid repetition
-   Schedule recovery rounds

Acceptance Criteria

-   Adaptive challenge selection.
-   Fair progression.
-   Configurable behavior.

End of Chapter 2.

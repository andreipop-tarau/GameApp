Animation and Motion Guidelines

13.1 Purpose

Motion enhances usability by communicating state changes, guiding
attention, and making the experience feel polished without slowing the
player down.

13.2 Motion Principles

-   Fast
-   Purposeful
-   Consistent
-   Responsive
-   Accessible

Animations should support interaction rather than distract from it.

13.3 Timing

Recommended durations: - Micro interactions: 100–150ms - Standard
transitions: 200–300ms - Reward animations: <600ms - Screen transitions:
<300ms

13.4 Easing

Preferred easing: - Ease Out - Ease In Out

Avoid elastic or exaggerated animations during gameplay.

13.5 Screen Transitions

Transitions should: - Preserve context - Avoid long fades - Never block
interaction

13.6 Micro-interactions

Animate: - Buttons - Cards - Progress bars - Shop purchases -
Achievement unlocks - Notifications

13.7 Haptic Feedback

Use haptics for: - Correct actions - Incorrect actions - Purchases -
Achievement unlocks - Level ups

Allow players to disable haptics.

13.8 Loading States

Prefer skeleton loaders or subtle indicators.

Avoid blocking the interface whenever possible.

13.9 Performance

Maintain: - Stable 60 FPS - Minimal dropped frames - GPU-friendly
animations

Acceptance Criteria

-   Animations remain smooth.
-   Motion improves clarity.
-   Gameplay is never delayed by animation.
-   Accessibility options support reduced motion.

Backup & Disaster Recovery

Purpose

Ensure data durability and rapid recovery.

Strategy

-   Automated database backups
-   Point-in-time recovery
-   Storage backups
-   Configuration backups

Recovery Goals

-   RPO: <15 minutes
-   RTO: <1 hour

Acceptance Criteria

-   Backups verified.
-   Recovery procedures documented.
-   Regular restore tests.

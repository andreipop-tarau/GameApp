Volume 2 - Chapter 8

Shop Screen

8.1 Purpose

The Shop Screen allows players to purchase optional cosmetics, Brain
Chips, bundles and convenience items. It must remain simple, transparent
and never feel mandatory.

8.2 Design Goals

-   Fast browsing
-   Clear prices
-   Large previews
-   Minimal purchase friction
-   No misleading offers

8.3 Layout

Top: - Brain Chips balance - Featured offer banner

Center: - Featured - Daily Shop - Themes - Frames - Particles - Badges -
Bundles - Founder (when active) - Seasonal

Bottom: - Navigation bar

8.4 Product Cards

Each card displays: - Preview image - Item name - Price - Rarity -
Limited tag (if applicable) - Owned indicator

8.5 Product Details

Selecting an item opens: - Large preview - Description - Collection -
Price - Purchase button - Preview animation

8.6 Purchase Flow

Browse → Preview → Confirm → Payment → Success Animation → Inventory
Updated

Purchases should complete in only a few seconds.

8.7 Daily Shop

Refresh every 24 hours.

Contains: - Common cosmetics - Rare cosmetics - Featured bundle -
Discounted item

8.8 Founder Section

Visible only during the Founder Event.

Displays: - Countdown - Founder Bundle - Founder cosmetics - Bonus Chip
promotion

8.9 Lucky Spin

One free spin each day.

Extra spins require Brain Chips.

Rewards: - Chips - Themes - Frames - Particles - Badges - Rare cosmetics

8.10 Inventory

Purchased items appear immediately.

Players can: - Equip - Preview - Filter - Favorite

8.11 Error Handling

If purchase fails: - Show clear error. - Retry safely. - Never duplicate
purchases.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Shop loads quickly.
-   Purchases are secure.
-   Inventory updates instantly.
-   Daily shop refreshes automatically.
-   Cosmetics never affect gameplay.

End of Volume 2 - Chapter 8.

MindTrap AI

Volume 1 - Chapter 1

Vision & Product Philosophy

1.1 Vision Statement

MindTrap AI is a mobile-first psychological challenge game designed for
iOS and Android. The experience is built around extremely short play
sessions (5–30 seconds) with instant restarts and unlimited free
gameplay. Unlike traditional games with handcrafted levels, MindTrap AI
continuously adapts to each player through an AI Director that selects
and combines challenge mechanics based on player behavior.

The long-term vision is to create a game that players open dozens of
times per day because every session feels fresh, fair, and
unpredictable.

1.2 Product Goals

Primary goals: - Instant access to gameplay. - Unlimited
replayability. - Fair competition. - Unlimited free play. -
Cosmetics-only monetization. - Long-term LiveOps support.

Every feature should increase retention, replayability, or
maintainability.

1.3 Target Audience

Primary audience: - Casual mobile players. - Ages 13+. - Players looking
for 5–30 second sessions. - Users who enjoy puzzles, reflex games, and
psychological challenges.

Secondary audience: - Competitive leaderboard players. - Cosmetic
collectors. - Daily challenge enthusiasts.

1.4 Core Design Pillars

1.  Instant Gameplay The player should be in a playable round within
    seconds after opening the app.

2.  One More Round Every completed round should naturally encourage
    another attempt.

3.  Adaptive AI Difficulty adapts to the individual player instead of
    following fixed levels.

4.  Unlimited Gameplay No energy systems, lives, or waiting timers.

5.  Fairness A free player can reach the top leaderboard without
    spending money.

1.5 Success Metrics

Product success should be measured using: - D1 retention above 45% - D7
retention above 20% - Average session length 8–20 minutes - Six or more
sessions per active day - Store rating above 4.7 - High replay rate -
Strong cosmetic conversion

1.6 Development Philosophy

Quality always takes priority over feature quantity.

Systems must be modular, scalable, and data-driven. No gameplay feature
should be tightly coupled to another. All balancing should be
configurable from the backend whenever possible.

The game should continuously evolve through LiveOps without requiring
frequent application updates.

Appendix A - Game Balance Bible

Purpose

Define numerical balancing guidelines.

XP

-   Easy: 10 XP
-   Medium: 20 XP
-   Hard: 35 XP
-   Daily Challenge: 50 XP

Brain Chips

Earn from: - Daily login - Events - Achievements - Referrals

Never award unlimited premium currency.

Shop Pricing

Common: 10–30 Chips Rare: 40–80 Chips Epic: 100–200 Chips Legendary:
250+ Chips

Difficulty Targets

Player success rate: 60–70% Average round: 5–30 seconds.

Acceptance Criteria

Economy remains fair and configurable.

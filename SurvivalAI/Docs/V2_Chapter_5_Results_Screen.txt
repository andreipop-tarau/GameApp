Volume 2 - Chapter 5

Results Screen

5.1 Purpose

The Results Screen provides immediate feedback after every challenge
while encouraging the player to begin another round as quickly as
possible.

5.2 Design Goals

-   Display results instantly.
-   Celebrate success.
-   Explain failures when appropriate.
-   Encourage “Play Again”.
-   Keep total display time under 3 seconds.

5.3 Layout

Top: - Win / Lose banner - Challenge summary

Center: - Score - XP earned - Brain Chips earned (if applicable) - Brain
Profile updates

Bottom: - Play Again (primary) - Share Result - Home

5.4 Rewards

Display: - XP gained - Level progress - Achievement unlocks - Mission
progress - Cosmetic unlocks - Event rewards

Reward animations should complete quickly and be skippable.

5.5 Brain Profile Updates

Show any changes to: - Reaction - Memory - Attention - Logic -
Prediction

Changes should be subtle and easy to understand.

5.6 Social Sharing

Players may share: - Personal best - Daily Challenge score - Brain
Type - Rare achievements

Generate a social-friendly image with one tap.

5.7 Play Again

The Play Again button is the primary action.

Requirements: - Largest button on screen. - Starts next round
immediately. - No confirmation dialog.

5.8 Performance

Target: - Results screen appears in under 300ms. - Reward animations
remain smooth at 60 FPS. - Next round loads instantly.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Results are shown immediately.
-   Rewards update correctly.
-   Play Again is always prominent.
-   Sharing works with minimal taps.
-   Restart flow remains frictionless.

End of Volume 2 - Chapter 5.

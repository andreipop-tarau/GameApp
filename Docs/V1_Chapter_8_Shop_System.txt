Shop System

8.1 Purpose

The Shop is the primary monetization feature. It must feel rewarding,
simple, and completely optional. Players should never feel forced to
spend money.

8.2 Design Principles

-   No Pay-to-Win
-   Cosmetic-first
-   Clear pricing
-   Fast purchasing
-   Minimal friction
-   Daily reasons to return

8.3 Shop Categories

-   Themes
-   Avatar Frames
-   Particle Effects
-   Name Effects
-   Badges
-   Sound Packs
-   Hint Tokens
-   Continue Tokens
-   Lucky Spins
-   Bundles

8.4 Shop Layout

Sections: - Featured - Daily Shop - Collections - Bundles - Founder
(during launch) - Seasonal - Owned Items

8.5 Daily Shop

Refreshes every 24 hours.

Contains: - 4 common cosmetics - 2 rare cosmetics - 1 featured item - 1
discounted bundle

Daily shop should encourage regular visits.

8.6 Bundles

Starter Bundle - 200 Chips - Theme - Frame - Badge

Founder Bundle - Exclusive cosmetics - Founder badge - Founder frame -
Founder particles

Season Bundle - Limited seasonal cosmetics

8.7 Lucky Spin

Players receive: - 1 free spin every day.

Additional spins cost Brain Chips.

Possible rewards: - Chips - Themes - Frames - Particles - Badges - Rare
cosmetics

8.8 Pricing Philosophy

Small purchases should feel meaningful.

Most cosmetics should cost between: 10–30 Brain Chips.

Premium cosmetics: 40–80 Brain Chips.

Bundles always provide better value.

8.9 Limited-Time Items

Holiday collections

Weekend specials

Founder exclusives

Season exclusives

Retired cosmetics should remain unavailable to preserve rarity.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Purchases complete in seconds.
-   Inventory updates immediately.
-   Shop content is remotely configurable.
-   Cosmetics never affect gameplay.
-   Daily shop rotates automatically.

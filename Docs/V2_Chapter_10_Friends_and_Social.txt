Volume 2 - Chapter 10

Friends and Social

10.1 Purpose

The Friends & Social screen helps players connect, compete, and share
their progress while maintaining privacy and fair competition.

10.2 Design Goals

-   Simple friend management
-   Friendly competition
-   Fast challenge sharing
-   Secure privacy controls
-   Cross-platform synchronization

10.3 Layout

Top: - Search friends - Friend requests - Invite friends

Center: - Friends list - Online status (future) - Recent activity

Bottom: - Challenge history - Referral shortcut

10.4 Friend Requests

Players can: - Send requests - Accept - Decline - Cancel pending
requests - Block users

10.5 Friend Profiles

Display: - Avatar - Username - Level - Brain Type - Favorite cosmetics -
Personal best - Public achievements

10.6 Challenges

Players may: - Send challenge links - Compare scores - View challenge
history - Rematch completed challenges

10.7 Referrals

Display: - Personal referral code - Share button - Invited friends -
Rewards earned

10.8 Privacy

Configurable options: - Public profile - Accept friend requests - Show
online status - Share Brain Profile - Appear on friend leaderboard

10.9 Error Handling

If synchronization fails: - Show cached friends. - Retry
automatically. - Never lose pending requests.

Acceptance Criteria

-   Friends sync across devices.
-   Challenge links open correctly.
-   Referral rewards validate server-side.
-   Privacy settings are enforced.
-   UI remains responsive.

End of Volume 2 - Chapter 10.

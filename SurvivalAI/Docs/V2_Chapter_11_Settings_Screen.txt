Volume 2 - Chapter 11

Settings Screen

11.1 Purpose

The Settings Screen allows players to customize their experience, manage
their account, configure accessibility, and control privacy without
affecting gameplay.

11.2 Design Goals

-   Simple organization
-   Fast access
-   Clear labels
-   Immediate feedback
-   Safe account management

11.3 Sections

-   Account
-   Audio
-   Graphics
-   Gameplay
-   Accessibility
-   Notifications
-   Privacy
-   Language
-   Support
-   About

11.4 Account

Players can: - Sign in/out - Link providers - Restore purchases - View
account ID - Delete account (server-confirmed)

11.5 Audio

Controls: - Master Volume - Music Volume - Sound Effects - Haptics -
Mute All

Changes apply immediately.

11.6 Graphics

Options: - Reduce Motion - High Performance Mode - Battery Saver - FPS
Display (Developer)

11.7 Gameplay

Options: - Left/Right handed preferences - Tutorial replay -
Auto-confirm dialogs - Practice defaults

11.8 Accessibility

Support: - Large Text - High Contrast - Colorblind Mode - Haptic
Toggle - Screen Reader Labels

11.9 Notifications

Players control: - Daily Challenge - Events - Friend Challenges - Shop
Refresh - Seasonal Alerts

11.10 Privacy

Options: - Public Profile - Friend Requests - Leaderboard Visibility -
Analytics Consent - Personalized Offers

11.11 Support

Provide: - FAQ - Contact Support - Report Bug - Terms of Service -
Privacy Policy

Acceptance Criteria

-   Settings synchronize across devices where applicable.
-   Changes apply immediately.
-   Sensitive actions require confirmation.
-   Accessibility is available from first launch.
-   Screen remains responsive and organized.

End of Volume 2 - Chapter 11.
